Know what you’re seeing

Freshness, not false precision.

Recent: survey started less than 5 minutes ago.
Stale: 5–15 minutes, so the count is hidden.
Unknown: no survey, or 15+ minutes old.
Level total: shown only when all of the level's bays are recent. Otherwise you see “≥ N in 2 of 3 bays”.

Age counts from when the survey started, because a walk takes minutes. Demo thresholds only: a pilot must measure how fast counts go out of date.

What is counted: open spaces a holder of the example general permit could use. Not counted: accessible (ADA), visitor, reserved, EV-only, motorcycle, blocked or coned spaces. An empty space is not always one you may use.

One bounded observation

Walk. Count. Complete.

Pick one bay. Start at zero, walk the whole bay once, and count only spaces that the example general permit may use. Don't mix partial walks or count a space twice.

Counting rules

  1. Count only empty spaces the example general permit may use. Skip accessible, visitor, reserved, EV-only, motorcycle and blocked spaces.
  2. A completed survey replaces the bay's old count. It never adds to it.
  3. A survey open more than 20 minutes is rejected. Start again.
  4. Partial or cancelled surveys never reach students.
  5. Zero means you found no usable open spaces. Unknown means no recent survey.

Optional fallback · simulated reports

Report a deck entry or exit

Shows how students could report if cameras aren't possible. Report only after you've parked or stopped, never while driving. This demo records no identity or real movement.

Low-trust reporting channel

Movement reports, not vacancies

Occupancy: unknown. Available spaces: unknown.
No verified starting count, full participation, capacity or exit coverage. These totals are never combined with staff surveys.

Missed reports cause drift; duplicate or false reports distort totals. A short repeat-tap guard is not identity checking. A real pilot would need a staff baseline count and regular reconciliation. A negative net total is possible and does not mean negative occupancy.

A credible one-deck pilot

Test the staff workflow and whether students understand the display before claiming to solve parking. Confirm with KSU Parking & Transportation: the real number of levels, eligible stalls per bay and permit type, and whether they would take part.

  • Built now: bay surveys, replace-not-add, timestamps from survey start, expiry, unknown states, level totals only when complete, local storage, and a 3D view.
  • 3D model: made from public USGS lidar and USDA aerial photos. It shows where bays are. It does not detect cars or open spaces.
  • Student backup: simulated entry/exit reports, kept separate as a low-trust signal. No QR codes yet.
  • Cameras: not connected. KSU's Video Monitoring Standard (Sept 2024) routes live viewing and new cameras through the Department of Public Safety.
  • Privacy: no plates, faces, student identity or enforcement records.
  • AI: this prototype runs no AI model. AI-assisted development is not an AI-powered product.

This prototype cannot reserve spaces, validate a permit, prevent tickets or detect accidents. Use it to test an idea, not to make parking decisions.
