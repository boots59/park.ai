Everything on your phone: the app at https://boots59.github.io/park.ai/ and the field kit (poll and circling tally)
at https://boots59.github.io/park.ai/field.html. Open both once on Wi-Fi before you go.

**Rules:** counts only. No photos of people. Photos of cars stay on your phone (they show plates). Stay out of
traffic lanes. The poll is anonymous: no names, no contact details.

### Three questions for a parking monitor
1. On a busy day, how often do you walk this deck, and do you cover every level?
2. Does your scanner show how many cars you have scanned on each level?
3. If you tapped "open spaces in this section" on a phone as you went, would that slow you down too much?

## What to send afterwards
1. App: Staff survey tab, "Export field log (CSV)".
2. Field kit: Results tab, "Export everything (CSV)".
3. Stall counts per level and bay, the number of levels, and what the monitor and Talon One said.
4. Not the photos.

## Reading the poll honestly
It is a convenience sample of people you happened to meet near one deck on one day, and the minutes are self-reported.
Say "of the N students I asked", never "N% of KSU students".
