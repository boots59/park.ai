Know what you’re seeing

Freshness, not false precision.

Recent: checked less than 5 minutes ago.
Stale: 5–15 minutes old, so the previous count is hidden.
Unknown: no survey, or 15+ minutes since the last check.

Demo thresholds only. A pilot must measure how quickly observations become unreliable. Empty does not mean eligible: permits and space restrictions still apply.

One bounded observation

Walk. Count. Complete.

Start a new pass at zero. Count only spaces available to the intended permit group. Finish the entire illustrative level once; do not mix partial passes or repeat previously counted spaces.

Counting rules

  1. Only count empty spaces that are eligible for the intended permit group. Exclude blocked or restricted spaces.
  2. A new completed survey replaces the old level count. It never adds to it.
  3. Partial or canceled surveys do not appear on the student dashboard.
  4. Zero means an observer found no eligible open spaces. Unknown means there is no sufficiently fresh observation.

Movement reports, not vacancies

Occupancy: unknown. Available spaces: unknown.
No verified starting occupancy, complete participation, capacity, or exit coverage. These totals are not combined with staff observations.

Missed reports create drift. Duplicate or incorrect reports can distort totals. A brief repeat-click guard is not identity verification or reliable deduplication. A real pilot needs reconciliation against independent counts; negative net reports are possible and do not mean negative occupancy.

A credible one-deck pilot

Validate staff workflow and student comprehension before claiming to solve parking. Confirm the actual West Deck levels, eligible stall counts, permit rules, and KSU’s willingness to participate.

  • Built now: bounded surveys, per-level replacement, timestamps, expiry, unknown states, and local persistence.
  • Student backup: simulated entry/exit reporting is implemented as a separate low-trust channel. QR distribution is not implemented. Missed or duplicate reports prevent treating these totals as occupancy or vacancies.
  • Cameras: not connected. Installation or access needs university authorization. Gate counts alone cannot locate a space or level.
  • Privacy: no plates, faces, client information, student identity, or enforcement records.
  • AI: this prototype does not run an AI model. AI-assisted development is not the same as an AI-powered product, and hackathon track eligibility remains unverified.

This prototype cannot reserve spaces, validate a permit, prevent tickets, provide insurance, or promise accident detection. Use it to test a product idea, not to make operational decisions.
