## What the cross-tabulations show
**1. The 9-to-11 arrival is the worst.**
| Arrived | Drivers | Took 6+ min | Tried 2+ levels | Late several times this semester |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before 9 | 10 | 1 | 4 | 4 |
| 9 to 11 | 14 | 8 | 10 | 9 |
| 11 to 1 | 8 | 4 | 6 | 4 |
| After 1 | 22 | 7 | 11 | 10 |

**2. Hunting between levels is where the time goes.** Of the 31 who tried more than one level, 17 took 6 minutes or more. Of the 23 who did not, 3 did (all three said "over 20", which may be a wait or a misread question).

**3. The more often you drive in, the more often you give up.** Gave up on a deck several times: 1 of 12 who drive 1–2 days, 8 of 27 who drive 3–4 days, 8 of 15 who drive 5 or more.

**4. It is not only West Deck.** The 15 who parked elsewhere: all 15 late at least once, 11 tried more than one level or lot, 7 took 6+ minutes. Small group, same pattern.

**5. The people hurt most want it most.** Of the 27 late several times, 23 would check the page most times and 21 would tap after parking. Of the 5 never late, 3 and 2.

**6. Almost everyone would change what they do.** With level information: 40 straight to that level, 8 a different deck or lot, 3 not sure, 3 nothing different. That is 48 of 54 who say their behaviour would change.

**7. The two groups agree.** In person vs QR: late at least once 27/30 vs 22/24; tried 2+ levels 16/30 vs 15/24; 6+ minutes 12/30 vs 8/24; straight to the level 20/30 vs 20/24. Two different collection methods giving the same picture is the strongest thing about this data.

**The skeptics.** 6 of 54 would not check the page; 3 would not tap. Four of those six still said they would have gone straight to a level with room.

## App reviews (9 testers, evening of Nov 19)
| Question | Positive | Middle | Negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy to use? | 7 yes | 2 sort of | 0 no |
| Would you check it before driving in? | 7 yes | 2 maybe | 0 no |
| Do you trust the numbers? | 5 yes | 4 somewhat | 0 no |

Most useful part: Levels 5, Now 3, 3D deck 1. Nobody gave a negative answer to any question.
**Trust is the weakest score**: 4 of 9 only "somewhat" trust the numbers. That is the correct reaction to a replay of one morning's count, and it is the thing a pilot with fresh counts would have to move.
Four written suggestions: compass-direction bay names are confusing (2), the 3D model's orientation is unclear (1), add a written-response option (1). The first three were acted on the same night (compass, level tags, landmark hint, entrances and the campus exit on the model).

## What this data cannot tell you
- **It is not a sample of KSU students.** These are people at or near one deck on one Thursday, the busiest kind of day, who agreed to answer. People with a parking grievance are more likely to stop.
- **Everything is self-reported from memory.** "Late this semester" and "minutes to park" were not observed.
- **"Would" is not "did".** Stated intent overstates real use. Nobody has yet parked using the app.
- **Nine testers were asked by the person who built it**, face to face. Expect politeness.
- **No time saving has been measured.**

## What can be said safely
- "Of 54 drivers I polled at West Deck, 49 said parking has made them late to class this semester, and half said several times."
- "One in three took six minutes or more to park that day; four took over twenty."
- "Three in four said they would have gone straight to a level shown to have room."
- Not: "91% of KSU students", "saves 10 minutes", or "students love the app".
