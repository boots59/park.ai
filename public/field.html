Stall count

Count painted stalls, one bay (or one row) at a time. Tap as you walk; your taps are kept if the page reloads. Save at the end of each bay.

Say: “Hi, I'm a student doing a 30-second anonymous poll about parking for a hackathon project. No names. Do you have a moment?”

Circling tally

Stand somewhere safe where you can see cars come onto one level. Tap once per car. “Left without parking” means it drove through the level and went on to another level or out.
