# Demo video: shot list (aim for 2:30, never over 3:00)

Rules that matter more than polish:
- **Show the app working in the first 20 seconds.** Do not open with a title card or your face talking.
- **One take per shot, phone screen recording** (iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording, microphone on). Record the voice at the same time; do not add music.
- **Say what is real.** The yellow bar says "A replay, not live". Say it once and move on.
- If a shot goes wrong, re-record only that shot. Join the clips in any free editor.

## Where to film shot 4
The camera scan needs real parked cars. Best: West Deck or any lot, standing at the end of a row with the phone held high. If you cannot get to a lot, point the phone at a photo of a parking lot on a laptop screen and say "I'm pointing it at a photo here".

## Before you record
- Do every shot once without recording, on the phone you will record on.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb. Brightness up. Close other tabs.
- In shot 4, allow the camera when the phone asks. Do it once beforehand so the prompt does not appear in the take.
