# Demo video: shot list (aim for 1:45, never over 2:30)

**Pattern taken from a first-place demo video** (82 seconds, watched frame by frame): logo card (5 s) → the problem shown with real people, not statistics (8 s) → a glimpse of the product and a tagline card (by 0:21) → one uninterrupted screen recording of the core workflow (about 45 s) → logo card out. No face, no slides, no feature tour, voice-over throughout with no silences.

Rules:
- **One story, in the order it happens.** Product on screen by 0:15.
- **Screen-record the phone** (iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording, microphone on). Record your voice at the same time, or record the voice separately reading the "Say" column and lay it over.
- **Keep talking.** The winning video has no silent gaps, except the two-second hold when the camera boxes the cars.
- **Cards are ready** in `submission/video-cards/`: `1-title.png`, `2-question.png`, `3-end.png`. Drop them into any free editor (CapCut, iMovie) between your clips.
- Say once that the app is replaying your real Thursday count.

**If you have room up to 2:30:** after shot 6 add **Ask** → "Where should I park?" → **Show me** (10 s), and after shot 7 add the before-and-after slide (15 s).

**Filming shot 2:** film from the passenger seat or on foot, never while driving. Keep licence plates and faces out of frame or too small to read.

## Where to film shot 2
The camera scan needs real parked cars. Best: West Deck or any lot, standing at the end of a row with the phone held high. If you cannot get to a lot, point the phone at a photo of a parking lot on a laptop screen and say "I'm pointing it at a photo here".

## Before you record
- Do every shot once without recording, on the phone you will record on.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb. Brightness up. Close other tabs.
- In shot 2, allow the camera when the phone asks. Do it once beforehand so the prompt does not appear in the take.
