# Demo video: shot list (aim for 1:50, never over 2:30)

**Built from five videos, watched frame by frame with the audio transcribed:**
- *Skyris (YC hackathon demo, 89 s)*: no title card. The first words are "We're both pilots and we hate these charts. It takes an hour", said over the painful thing itself. Product on screen at 0:12, one unbroken screen recording of one journey, and it ends on traction: "we reached out to 250 pilots, seven loved it".
- *Student Tasker (first place, 82 s)*: logo, problem, product, logo. One workflow, voice-over the whole time.
- *Pitch Perfect (first place, live)*: a personal story up front. The live demo broke for 25 seconds and he recovered with a joke; a judge then asked him to "do a really bad one". Have the bad case ready.
- *Unicorn Mafia ($100k winners)*: one belief in one sentence ("vacations shouldn't be a project") and real users during the hackathon.
- *A hackathon judge*: why, then how, then what. Do not restate the problem you were given; say how you see it. Be specific and brave about how narrow it is.

**So yours:** open on the pain in your own words, product by 0:12, one journey, end on your real numbers.

Rules:
- **One story, in the order it happens.** Product on screen by 0:15.
- **Screen-record the phone** (iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Recording, microphone on). Record your voice at the same time, or record the voice separately reading the "Say" column and lay it over.
- **Keep talking.** The winning video has no silent gaps, except the two-second hold when the camera boxes the cars.
- **Cards are ready** in `submission/video-cards/`: `1-title.png`, `2-question.png`, `3-end.png`. Drop them into any free editor (CapCut, iMovie) between your clips.
- Say once that the app is replaying your real Thursday count.

**If you have room up to 2:30:** after shot 6 add **Ask** → "Where should I park?" → **Show me** (10 s), and after shot 7 add the before-and-after slide (15 s).

**Filming shot 1:** film from the passenger seat or on foot, never while driving. Keep licence plates and faces out of frame or too small to read.

## Where to film shot 3
The camera scan needs real parked cars. Best: West Deck or any lot, standing at the end of a row with the phone held high. If you cannot get to a lot, point the phone at a photo of a parking lot on a laptop screen and say "I'm pointing it at a photo here".

## Before you record
- Do every shot once without recording, on the phone you will record on.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb. Brightness up. Close other tabs.
- In shot 3, allow the camera when the phone asks. Do it once beforehand so the prompt does not appear in the take.
