## How to add your voice (10 minutes)
1. Open the video in CapCut or iMovie (phone or laptop).
2. Tap **Voiceover** / **Record**, press play, and read the lines as the captions change. Short sentences, steady pace, no rush: the lines are written to fit.
3. If you fluff a line, re-record only that part.
4. Export at 1080p. Name it `Park-AI-demo.mp4`.

No editor? Play the video on a laptop and record the screen with your microphone on (QuickTime or the Windows Game Bar), reading as it plays.
