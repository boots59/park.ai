# Demo script (2 minutes)

Use "Reset sample data" before starting. Say "sample data" out loud once.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Students tab, top of page | "This is what a student sees before driving in. Sample data." |
| 0:15 | Point at the verdict, then Level 2's range | "A fresh count is a number. An older count becomes a range. Nothing stale is shown as fact." |
| 0:35 | Tap a bay on the 3D model (if included) | "Each bay shows when it was counted and whether it was filling." |
| 0:50 | Staff survey tab: pick a bay, Start | "A parking monitor on their normal round." |
| 1:00 | Photograph a section (use a prepared photo) | "The model counts vehicles on the phone. The photo is never saved." |
| 1:20 | Correct the number by one, Use suggestion, Complete | "The monitor is in charge. Every correction is logged, which is how we measure the AI." |
| 1:35 | Back to Students: the bay is now "Counted" | "The new count replaced the old one." |
| 1:45 | Advance 6 minutes | "Six minutes later it is already a range again." |
| 1:55 | Field log summary | "And here is the model's measured accuracy from Thursday." |

Fallback: if the AI step fails live, play the recorded video from 1:00.
