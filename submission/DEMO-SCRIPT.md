# Live demo: one story, in order (2 minutes)

The story is the one from slide 1: **it is 11:50 on a Thursday and you have class at 12:15.** Show three things only: the camera count, the three-tap park flow, and the "You parked here" pin.

Before you start, on the phone you will demo with: open https://boots59.github.io/park.ai/, go to **Count** and enter the password **monitor** once, then return to **Now**. The yellow bar says "A replay, not live": say once that it replays your real Thursday count.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Count** → **West Deck** → **Level 3** → **West** | "First, how the numbers get in. A parking monitor, already walking the deck, picks the deck, the level and the bay. Three taps." |
| 0:15 | **Scan with camera · AI** → point at a row of cars. **Say nothing for two seconds when the boxes appear.** | "They point the phone down the row. The model counts every vehicle, on the phone. No plates, nothing recorded." |
| 0:35 | **Count this view** → use the number → **Save** | "They check it and save. The model on screen updates at once." |
| 0:50 | **Now** | "Now you, at 11:50, before you leave home. Fifty-six to eighty-three spaces open. And one question: where do you want to park?" |
| 1:05 | Tap the gold bay | "One tap. Here it is in the deck: Level 2, west bay, 22 open." |
| 1:15 | **I parked here** | "You park, and tap once. It tells you the exit to campus is on Level 3." |
| 1:25 | **A few** | "One more tap tells the next driver how full it is. Anonymous." |
| 1:35 | Back on **Now**: "You parked in Level 2 · West bay" → **Show me on the deck** | "And it remembers where you left your car." |
| 1:50 | Hold up the QR slide | "Scan this and try it yourself, right now." |

**If you have 20 spare seconds:** on **Deck**, tap **Level 1** to show it has no student stalls, or tap **Ask** → "Where should I park?".

**The camera step needs real cars in view.** Live on stage, point the phone at a photo of a parking lot on a second screen and say so.

**A "bad case" if a judge asks for one:** on **Deck**, tap Level 2 → **Centre** (0 open, with 38 faculty stalls), or open the `?live` link to show an old count labelled "not live".

**Fallback:** if the phone has no signal or the camera fails, play the recorded video from the same point in the story.
